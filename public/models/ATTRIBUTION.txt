Modifications
-------------
All models were optimised for web delivery: geometry quantisation
(KHR_mesh_quantization), texture resizing and WebP compression, pruning of
geometry not visible in game, and mesh joining. The tree pack additionally ships
only its five tall atlas-mapped variants; its detailed trees and rocks are not
included. No model's licence is changed by these modifications.

Note on the car licence
-----------------------
CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 prohibits commercial use. This project cannot be monetised while
that model ships. Replacing it with a permissively licensed or original vehicle is
a prerequisite for any commercial release.
